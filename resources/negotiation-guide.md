# Guide: Negotiation and Pushback (Client Role-Play)

FDEs negotiate scope daily: with customers who want everything by Friday, and
with internal product/eng who can't build it all.

**A reliable sequence**
1. **Clarify the real need** — ask what problem the request solves, who's
   asking, and what deadline actually matters before you react to the ask.
2. **Make the trade-off visible** — scope, timeline, quality: pick two. Offer
   options ("X by Friday, or X+Y in three weeks"), not a flat yes or no.
3. **Say "not yet," not just "no"** — name what you'd do instead and the
   condition that would change your answer (data access, a pilot result).
4. **Name the risk of saying yes** — what breaks if you overcommit; protect
   the first deliverable and agree when to revisit the rest.
5. **Escalate cleanly** — when you can't resolve it, take both views upward
   with a recommendation; tell the stakeholder first so it's no surprise.

**Signals they want:** staying calm and curious under pressure; keeping the
relationship while holding a line; confirming the agreement out loud.
